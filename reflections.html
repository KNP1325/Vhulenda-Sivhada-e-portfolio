REFLECTION

Reflections & Lessons Learnt

Every tutorial provides an opportunity to learn, improve and grow.

01

Communication

I have learnt that technical knowledge alone is not enough. A tutor must be able to explain concepts in a way that students can understand.

02

Patience

Students learn at different speeds. Patience allows me to provide additional explanations without making students feel uncomfortable about asking questions.

03

Adaptability

Different students may need different explanations or examples. I have learnt to adapt my approach when necessary.

04

Leadership

Tutoring has helped me develop leadership skills by encouraging students, organising learning activities and supporting their progress.

05

Deeper Understanding

Teaching a concept requires a deeper understanding of it. Explaining problems to others has strengthened my own subject knowledge.

06

Student Independence

One of the most important lessons is that students should gradually become confident enough to solve problems independently.

“

A successful tutorial is one where students leave with more confidence than when they arrived.

”

Vhulenda Sivhada | Strength of Materials 3 Tutor
