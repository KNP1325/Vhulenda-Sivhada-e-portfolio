Training & Development

MY DEVELOPMENT

Growing as an Academic Tutor

Participating in academic tutor orientation and continuous training sessions at iYunivesithi Walter Sisulu has helped me strengthen my instructional techniques and develop my confidence as a tutor.

These development opportunities have helped me understand that effective tutoring is not only about knowing the subject. It is also about communication, patience, inclusion, ethics and helping students become independent learners.

01

Pedagogical Skills

Learning active listening strategies and facilitating guided problem-solving rather than giving students immediate answers.

This allows students to think through a problem and develop their own problem-solving skills.

02

Inclusive Learning

Adapting my communication style to support students with different levels of preparation, understanding and confidence.

I aim to create an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and participating in tutorials.

03

Academic Integrity & Ethics

Supporting students while ensuring that the work they submit remains their own independently completed work.

As a tutor, I provide guidance and explanations without completing academic work on behalf of students.

04

Communication Skills

Developing clearer ways of explaining engineering concepts, formulas, diagrams and calculations.

Good communication helps students understand difficult concepts without feeling overwhelmed.

05

Guided Problem Solving

Helping students identify what is given, determine what is required and select an appropriate method before solving a problem.

06

Continuous Development

Reflecting on tutorial sessions and identifying areas where my teaching approach can be improved.

TRAINING EXPERIENCE

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Tutor orientation and training have provided opportunities to develop practical skills that can be applied during Strength of Materials 3 tutorials.

I have learned the importance of preparing before tutorials, listening to students, identifying areas of difficulty and adjusting explanations when necessary.
