MY PURPOSE

Helping Students Understand Engineering

Strength of Materials 3 can be challenging because students are required to understand theoretical concepts while also applying calculations, diagrams and engineering principles.

My goal as a tutor is to make difficult concepts easier to understand by explaining them step by step and encouraging students to participate in solving problems.

Understanding the method is just as important as getting the correct answer.
