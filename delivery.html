TUTORIALS

Tutorial Delivery

My tutorials focus on understanding, participation, problem solving and independent learning.

My Tutorial Approach

  1. Understand

    Introduce the concept and explain its purpose.

  2. Identify

    Identify the information provided by the problem.

  3. Select

    Select the appropriate formula or method.

  4. Solve

    Work through the calculation step by step.

  5. Interpret

    Explain what the final answer means.

  6. Practise

    Give students opportunities to practise independently.

During Tutorials

01

Explain

Break difficult engineering concepts into smaller, understandable steps.

02

Demonstrate

Work through examples while explaining the reasoning behind each step.

03

Engage

Encourage students to ask questions and participate during tutorials.

04

Practise

Allow students to solve similar problems themselves and provide guidance where necessary.
